About Me

Growing up, I’ve always been fascinated by the idea of changing the world through technology. One moment in particular shaped that perspective.

When I was about 11, my father pointed out a set of traffic lights and asked me if I had ever thought about how they worked. I hadn’t. Then he told me: “They run on software.”

That simple idea changed how I saw the world. From that point on, I stopped viewing technology as something that simply existed and started questioning how and why things worked. I became curious, analytical, and driven to understand the systems behind everyday life.

That curiosity eventually grew into a passion for software development. A passion that continues to push me to build, learn, and solve real-world problems.
