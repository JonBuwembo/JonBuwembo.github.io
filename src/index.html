About Me

Growing up, I've always been fascinated about a way I can change the world through technology. As a child, I remember one day when my father was driving me to school and he asked me, "Jonathan! look at those traffic lights!"

I asked him, "What about them?" while I thought in my head they always change color.

"HMM" he responded, "Have you ever thought about how the lights change?"

I thought about it for a moment and then I said, "No, I guess I haven't." how would I know? I was barely 11 years old?

"Well ... they use software! There are programs running those lights!"

And from that point onward, I looked at the world differently, not just as an observer who assumed everything worked just as they were supposed to, but as a problem solver, and an inquirer who constantly seeked to understand HOW and WHY things worked. My passion for software development was born from that moment.

... And ever since, I never turned back.
